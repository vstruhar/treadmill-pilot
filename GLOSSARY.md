# Treadmill training

The language for planning a run on the treadmill and for what happened when you did it.

## Language

**Workout**:
A saved plan you can run again. An ordered list of Intervals.
_Avoid_: Program, training, routine

**Interval**:
One step of a Workout. It has a Stage, a duration, and a target speed.
_Avoid_: Segment, step, split

**Stage**:
The kind of Interval: Warm up, Training, Recover, or Cool down.
_Avoid_: Zone, type, phase

**Run**:
One time you performed a Workout, including when you stopped it early, and the stats from that time.
_Avoid_: Session, activity, log

**Cue**:
A sound that tells the runner the next Interval is coming: a spoken announcement, then countdown beeps.
_Avoid_: Alert, prompt, notification

**Treadmill**:
The one remembered machine the app controls.
_Avoid_: Device, belt, machine
